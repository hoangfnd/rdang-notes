# Welcome

Hi, I'm **Ryan Dang**.

Welcome to my personal knowledge base and digital notebook.

This website is where I document what I learn, build, troubleshoot, and discover throughout my professional and personal journey. It serves as a place to organize thoughts, preserve knowledge, and share experiences that may be useful to others.

## Who Am I?

I am an IT professional with a strong interest in:

- IT Operations
- System Administration
- Infrastructure & Virtualization
- Cloud Technologies
- Performance Analysis & Troubleshooting
- Automation & Productivity

Outside of work, I also enjoy learning about:

- Personal Finance & Investing
- Technology Products
- Consumer Electronics
- Continuous Learning and Self-Improvement

## What You'll Find Here

This blog contains notes, guides, observations, and lessons learned from both work and daily life.

Topics may include:

- Technical troubleshooting and root cause analysis
- Infrastructure and homelab projects
- Virtualization platforms such as Proxmox
- Application performance monitoring and optimization
- Cloud and automation tools
- Investing and personal finance
- Technology reviews and comparisons
- Productivity tips and learning notes

Some articles are detailed technical references, while others are simply personal reflections and experiences.

## Why I Created This Blog

Knowledge is easy to lose if it is not documented.

The primary goals of this blog are to:

- Record what I learn
- Build a personal knowledge repository
- Keep track of solutions to problems I've solved
- Share useful information with others
- Continuously improve through writing and reflection

Many of these articles are written for my future self, but I hope they can help anyone facing similar challenges or pursuing similar interests.

## Disclaimer

The content published on this website reflects my personal opinions, experiences, and understanding at the time of writing. Technology, financial markets, and best practices evolve over time, so readers should always conduct their own research and verification where appropriate.

Thank you for visiting.
